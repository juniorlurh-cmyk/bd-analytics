Our platform

Civix Analytics — intelligence you can cite

Most AI analytics tools will happily invent a statistic. Civix can't. A deterministic engine computes every number from the General Household Survey 2024; an LLM agent reasons over it; a verifier rejects any claim that doesn't trace back to a real result.

About

Built by people who measure what matters

BD Analytics is a South African data-intelligence practice. We work where rigorous analysis meets real-world decisions — for clients who need dashboards they can trust, geospatial answers for the ground they want to develop, and AI that cites its sources.

Our flagship platform, Civix Analytics, began with a simple conviction: national statistics deserve better than a language model guessing numbers. Every figure it reports is computed, weighted and verified.
